VSVS LLC owns a few houses in and around Scottsdale and rents them out. Everything to do with them goes through one address, hello@vsvsllc.com.

About

VSVS LLC is an Arizona limited liability company based in Scottsdale. It buys single-family homes in and around the city, keeps them, and rents them on standard Arizona residential leases.

There is no management company in between. The owner signs the leases and reads the email.

Tenants

If you rent one of our homes, email hello@vsvsllc.com for anything to do with the house: repairs, rent, the lease, a notice. Include the address and what happened.

If it is an emergency, such as fire, a gas smell or flooding, call 911 first and email afterwards.
