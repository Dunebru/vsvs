Speakeasy

Two words, thirty seconds, a talk that connects them. Speakeasy is a daily drill for the part of speaking nobody practises: thinking while people are listening.

Everything runs on the phone. Your voice is transcribed on the device, your talks stay on the device, and the app tells you what happened rather than grading you: how long you took to start, where you paused, which words you reached for.
