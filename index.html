Houses acrossArizona.

VSVS LLC owns houses in all parts of Arizona and rents them out. One owner, no management company, and one address for everything to do with them.

About

VSVS LLC is an Arizona limited liability company. It buys single-family houses, keeps them, and rents them on standard Arizona residential leases.

There is no management company in between. The owner signs the leases and reads the email.

Tenants

If you rent one of the houses, email hello@vsvsllc.com for anything about it: repairs, rent, the lease, a notice. Include the address and what happened.

If it is an emergency, such as fire, a gas smell or flooding, call 911 first and email afterwards.
