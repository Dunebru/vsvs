What VSVS is

A small property company. It owns a handful of homes in Scottsdale, lets them on plain terms, and keeps them properly.

There is no management company in between. The person who signs the lease is the person who owns the house, and the person who reads the email. Small by design: we would rather keep a few homes well than many badly.

How we work

Four things you can count on, in writing, before you sign.

Ownership

Every home we let is one we own. No sub-letting, no third-party managers, no call centre.

Repairs

Urgent things are seen to the same day. Everything else is scheduled within the week and done once, properly, by trades we would use in our own homes.

Terms

Standard Arizona residential leases and no surprise fees. Deposits come back on the timeline the law sets, with an itemised statement if anything is held.

Reach

All of the homes are in and around Scottsdale, close enough to visit. One email address for everything, and a person answers it.

Tenants

If something needs fixing, say so.

Email with the house and what happened. Urgent things the same day, everything else within the week. Rent questions, lease questions and notices go to the same address.
